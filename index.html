Flavour lines
One line per quote. Quotes live in three lists: [good] for wins and rallying lines, [bad] for losses and disasters, and [any] for neutral ones. By default Combat engaged and Stand down use [good], Cascade, Meltdown and Destroyed use [bad], and New round uses [any]. To give one animation its own lines, add a heading for it: [round] [cascade] [melt] [dead] [combat] [off]. An empty heading uses the default list. Lines with no heading go in [any].
